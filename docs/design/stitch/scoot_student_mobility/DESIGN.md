## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a welcoming, safety-centric, and vibrant visual language tailored for university students across India. Moving away from utilitarian transit spreadsheets and cold logistics interfaces, the visual direction merges high-contrast editorial clarity with warm, approachable tactile geometry inspired by modern mobile lifestyle platforms.

### Personality & Demographics
- **Target Audience:** Indian undergraduate and postgraduate students navigating tier-1 and tier-2 campus clusters (IITs, DU, Christ University, Manipal, Symbiosis, etc.) needing reliable daily commutes, weekend transit to transit hubs, and safe late-night ride-pooling.
- **Emotional Resonance:** Peer-to-peer trust, safety via verified institutional credentials (AISHE ID & Digilocker), relief from exorbitant cab surges, and cheerful campus camaraderie.
- **Visual Style:** Soft Pop Neo-Tactile. Defined by a serene lavender/lilac environmental canvas (`#E9E4FA`), high-radiance pure white card bodies (`#FFFFFF`) with sweeping rounded silhouettes (`2rem`–`2.5rem`), bold organic category surfaces (sunny ochre, powder sky, bubblegum pink), and floating obsidian pill navigators with stark contrasting active elements.

## Colors

The palette directly adapts the soft, youthful energy seen in modern lifestyle UI while meeting strict accessibility standards for Indian transit environments.

### Core Canvas & Foundation
- **Canvas / Environment:** `#E9E4FA` (Soft lilac/lavender canvas grounding the device frame).
- **Surface Elevation 0 (Primary Card Sheet):** `#FFFFFF` (Pure white base holding cards, lists, and forms).
- **Surface Contrast (Floats & Pills):** `#12141A` (Deep carbon obsidian for bottom floating docks and active day-state pills).

### Tactile Accent Containers & Identifiers
- **Lavender Accent (Primary / Verification):** `#7C5CE6` (Deep iris lilac for verification shield badges, verified student status, and active ride CTA elements).
- **Sunny Ochre (Carpool / Route Active):** `#FFA940` (Warm amber card fill indicating peak departure times, driver offerings, and seat availability).
- **Powder Sky (Scheduled / College Shuttle):** `#A8C7FA` (Calm sky blue for recurring college-gate pickups, metro feeder rides, and daily schedules).
- **Bubblegum Lilac/Pink (Split Cost & Social):** `#F7B5E6` (Playful soft pink for fair-share cost calculator tags, instant UPI splits, and co-passenger chips).
- **Emerald Safe (Security Status):** `#22C55E` (Live AISHE / Digilocker verification rings and GPS route guard beacons).

### Neutral Scale
- **Text Main:** `#12141A` (Deep neutral black with soft warmth for ultra-legible headlines).
- **Text Secondary:** `#666C7E` (Balanced neutral slate for route stops, timestamps, and metadata).
- **Text Inverse:** `#FFFFFF` (Pristine white on dark floating docks and saturated badges).
- **Surface Subtle:** `#F3F0FC` (Tonal lavender-tinted gray for inset chips and inactive day counters).
- **Border / Divider:** `rgba(18, 20, 26, 0.06)` (Ghost hairpins separating route waypoint steps).

## Typography

The type system uses **Plus Jakarta Sans** across all levels. Its geometric proportions, wide counters, and rounded apexes generate a youthful, friendly feel while maintaining sharp legibility on handheld screens under direct sunlight.

### Typographic Hierarchy
- **Headlines (`headline-lg`, `headline-md`):** Deep tracking (`-0.02em`), tight line spacing, and heavy weights (`700`/`800`). Used for daily greetings ("Hello, Sandra"), section declarations ("Your plan", "Ride matches"), and destination callouts.
- **Numeric & Pricing (`currency-lg`):** Configured specifically for Indian Rupee (`₹`) notation, pairing tabular figures with bold weights to make per-seat cost breakdowns transparent at a glance.
- **Metadata & Subtitles (`body-sm`, `label-md`):** Crisp medium weight (`500`–`600`) in `#666C7E` for pickup locations, gate identifiers, and vehicle specifications (e.g., "Gate 3 • Honda Activa • 2 seats left").

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around mobile-first ergonomics. Content lives on an elevated, expansive white card deck hovering inside a gentle lilac canvas background (`#E9E4FA`).

### Grid & Form Factors
- **Mobile Viewport (360px - 430px):** Single vertical column. Outer canvas margin is `1.25rem` (`margin`). Internal card container padding is `1.25rem` (`space-lg`), giving interactive tiles room to breathe.
- **Card Matrix:** Two-column asymmetric cards (`gutter: 0.75rem`) for quick glanceable options like split costs, scheduled rides, and active driver profiles.
- **Safe Area Padding:** Bottom clearance is fixed at `6rem` (`96px`) to ensure the floating navigation bar does not occlude route cards or booking triggers.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids heavy skeuomorphic drops or high-contrast drop-shadows. Depth is generated through **tonal stacking, soft ambient shadows, and pilliform overlays**:

1. **Environmental Ground (Elevation 0):** The soothing lilac canvas (`#E9E4FA`) operates as the foundational base.
2. **Main Floating App Sheet (Elevation 1):** Large continuous white card surfaces (`#FFFFFF`) with generous border curves (`2.5rem`) resting with a subtle diffused ambient shadow: `box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -8px rgba(124, 92, 230, 0.08)`.
3. **Tactile Color Blocks (Elevation 2):** Solid pastel color containers (`#FFA940`, `#A8C7FA`, `#F7B5E6`) have zero shadow, relying on pure flat color contrast against white surfaces.
4. **Floating Action Pills & Navigation (Elevation 3):** The dark bottom navigation dock (`#12141A`) sits floating above the content with an ambient drop: `box-shadow: 0 16px 36px rgba(18, 20, 26, 0.18)`. Active indicators inside this dock use crisp white pills that elevate via crisp visual inversion.

## Shapes

The design system embraces an ultra-rounded, soft-geometry aesthetic (`roundedness: 3`) reflecting ease of use, friendliness, and tactile comfort:

- **App Shell & Main Containers:** `2.5rem` (`40px`) radii create pebble-smooth edges on major surface containers.
- **Action Modules & Segment Tiles:** `1.75rem`–`2rem` (`28px`–`32px`) radii for category cards (Ochre Ride Card, Sky Blue Schedule Card).
- **Date & Filter Chips:** Full stadium pills (`9999px`) for daily calendar date selectors, filter tags, and ride-status labels.
- **Avatars & Visual Badges:** Precise geometric circles (`100%`) accented with 2px borders in primary lilac or verification emerald.

## Components

### 1. Floating Bottom Dock Navigation
- **Container:** Floating horizontal capsule (`#12141A`), positioned `1.5rem` above the bottom screen safe area, width spanning `calc(100% - 2.5rem)`, padding `0.5rem 0.75rem`, radius `9999px`.
- **Active State:** Pure white stadium capsule (`#FFFFFF`) encompassing the active icon in neutral black (`#12141A`), creating high focal contrast.
- **Inactive States:** Subtle light slate icons (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.6)`) evenly spaced, responding with gentle scale on tap.

### 2. Calendar / Day-Picker Strip
- **Container:** Horizontal row of 7 capsule pills resting on a muted lavender-gray tray (`#F3F0FC`).
- **Inactive Day Pill:** White or transparent capsule displaying 3-letter abbreviated day label (`body-sm`, `#666C7E`) and bold date numeral (`label-lg`, `#12141A`).
- **Active Day Pill:** High-contrast obsidian pill (`#12141A`), inverted white text (`#FFFFFF`), topped with a distinct circular focus indicator dot (`#FFFFFF`).

### 3. Tactile Ride & Trip Cards
- **Ochre Driver Offer Card:** Warm sunny background (`#FFA940`), housing destination header, pickup timeframe, vehicle type, and an embedded trainer/driver row with pill tag ("Medium", "Instant Confirmed").
- **Sky Blue Recurring Feeder Card:** Powder blue background (`#A8C7FA`), showcasing campus gate destinations, departure window ("18:00–19:30"), seat inventory, and playful tactile 3D route graphics.
- **Pink Social / Cost Share Capsule:** Soft pink accent (`#F7B5E6`) holding circular social link icons, passenger group avatars, and instant UPI split indicators.

### 4. Student Verification Marker & Trust Chips
- **AISHE Verified Badge:** Pill tag with iris purple outline (`#7C5CE6`), subtle tinted fill (`rgba(124, 92, 230, 0.08)`), containing a shield icon and verified college handle (e.g., "IIT Bombay • Verified Student").
- **Avatar Cluster:** Overlapping circular profile photos with `2px` white borders and an attached `+N` co-passenger count circle.

### 5. Input Fields & Ride Search
- **Search & Filter Bar:** Pure white pill surface with ghost border (`rgba(18, 20, 26, 0.08)`), circular leading icon button, placeholder in `#666C7E`, and trailing filter shortcut pill.
- **Fare Display:** Prominent `₹` symbol in `currency-lg` accompanied by a "Per Seat" sub-label and transparent formula breakdown modal ("Fair Split: Fuel + Toll / 3").
